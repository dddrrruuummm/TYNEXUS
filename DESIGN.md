# TY Nexus visual direction

## Concept
Hardware photographed like industrial design, not cloud software. TY Nexus is the connection between a compute brief and the machine, term, and supporting rack infrastructure that fit it. Borrow the direct scale of a hardware launch poster and pair it with clear enterprise buying information.

## Signature elements
1. Custom interlocking bridge mark: two open paths form a compact nexus, with a shared diagonal connection. Full wordmark plus independent icon/favicon.
2. A real server-room photograph across the first viewport with a large compressed company name; no split hero or decorative card.
3. A functional ownership selector: server purchase, compute rental, dedicated-server lease. It changes the relevant brief requirements and contact link rather than pretending to offer an instant company quote.
4. An equipment index combining consistent 4:3 photographs with inquiry-specific technical checklists, no shopping controls.

## Visual system
Anton display and Work Sans text, self-hosted with SIL OFL. Zero letter spacing and breakpoint-based fixed text sizes. White and graphite provide product contrast; deep bottle green is confined to selected surfaces, pale yellow gives the ownership selector a distinct field, and muted orange-red is reserved for small active accents. No gradient blobs, violet, glass cards, repeated icon-feature rows, negative tracking, or speculative metrics.

## Design guidance used
Taste Skill: technical brief draw, seed 1440283456, borrowed-category direction. The borrowed category is industrial-design exhibition. Read type, layout, copy and imagery references. Earlier everyday-goods draw was discarded when the owner confirmed the server industry. Dealt full-green/gold page is translated to neutral product surfaces to avoid a one-note palette; photos stay true to hardware, not the unrelated hands-and-making bundle. Font pair retained. Motion is a single modest hero settling movement plus feedback; no forced animation count.

Impeccable: read new-work, init, craft-floor guidance; use direct planning and bounded critique. Do not run the optional downloadable binary engine or persistent decision server because the workspace prohibits automatically enabling them. No telemetry, hooks, private uploads, or browser-inspector workaround.

Reference library: IBM for enterprise hierarchy and square utility, Apple for accurate hardware presentation, Wise for decisive contrast between neutral and colored surfaces. No brand assets, proprietary lettering, or copied layouts used.

## Imagery truth
Stock photographs and generated equipment are category visuals, not evidence of inventory, facilities or manufacturer authorization. Source/license and generation records stay in internal documentation. Never caption a stock server room as our data center. Catalog photographs must be distinct from home visuals and from one another.
